AC Leak Water Damage in Chester, PA

Condensate lines clog quietly. By the time a ceiling stains, the water has been running a while.

☎ (856) 702-0892

An air conditioner pulls water out of the air as it cools. That water, called condensate, drains through a line to a pan or outside. When the line clogs, the pan overflows and the water ends up in the ceiling, wall or floor around the air handler. A stain under an attic unit or a closet air handler is the usual first sign.

AC leak water damage repair in Chester, PA covers the water side of the problem. An HVAC technician clears or repairs the line. We remove wet insulation and materials, dry the structure and restore finishes.

Where AC leaks come from

Most leaks trace to one of four problems: a clogged condensate drain line, a cracked or rusted drain pan, a frozen evaporator coil that melts, or a failed pump on a unit that drains upward. Units in attics and closets cause the worst damage because the water drips onto drywall and insulation without anyone seeing it.

Ductwork can also leak when cold air meets warm, humid air and forms condensation on the outside of the duct. Wet insulation around ducts is a common attic finding.

Professional drying of a wet ceiling or closet follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, so ask whoever you hire whether their crews work to it.

What to do when you find water

  • Turn off the systemSwitch the thermostat to off and cut power to the air handler so it stops making more water.
  • Catch and drainPlace a bucket under a drip. A shop vacuum on the drain line cleanout may clear a clog.
  • Do not run it againWait for an HVAC technician. Running a clogged unit repeats the leak.
  • Photograph the stainInclude the unit, the pan and the ceiling below.

This is usually a slow leak, so the damage is not always an emergency. If a ceiling is sagging or dripping heavily, call dispatch right away. The same 24/7 line is open.

Cleanup and drying

Wet insulation is removed because it holds water. Drywall that stays wet is cut back and the framing is dried with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. When the leak is slow, mold is the main risk, so the crew checks moisture readings and looks for staining before closing up.

Why slow AC leaks matter

A leak that drips slowly for a summer can saturate insulation and rot framing without a visible stain. Rust marks below a wall-mounted unit, a musty smell, or a ceiling stain that returns after painting are all signs the source was never fixed.

Ask your HVAC technician to inspect the drain line, pan and any float switch, and ask whether a safety switch can shut the unit off if the pan fills.

Why attics and closets cause the worst damage

A drain pan under an attic air handler is the only thing between condensate and the ceiling below. When it overflows, the water lands on insulation and drywall that nobody looks at until a stain appears. Closet units over finished floors do similar damage to carpet and baseboards.

Summers in the Philadelphia region are hot and humid, so air conditioners run long hours and make a lot of condensate. Algae and dust build up inside the drain line over the season, which is why clogs often show up in July and August.

Ask your HVAC technician about a secondary drain pan and a float switch. The switch cuts power to the unit when water backs up, so a clog costs you a cooling day instead of a ceiling. That repair is the technician's job, and drying the damage that already happened is ours.

Cost factors and insurance

  • How long it leaked. A single overflow is small, a season of drips is not.
  • Insulation. Wet attic insulation is replaced.
  • Ceiling finish. Textured and high ceilings cost more to repair.
  • Access. Attic crawl space and tight closets add labor.

Common questions

Do you fix the air conditioner?

No. An HVAC technician repairs the unit and the drain. We handle water removal, drying and restoration of the damaged materials.

Can an AC leak cause mold?

Yes, if materials stay damp. That is why moisture readings matter even after a stain looks dry.

Is water from an AC clean?

Condensate is not sewage, but it can carry dust, algae and rust. Dry it promptly.

Do you handle AC leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Chester?

Dispatch is open 24 hours a day. A dripping or sagging ceiling is the case to call about at once.

Why does my ceiling stain return after painting?

The source is still leaking or the cavity is still wet. Paint will not fix either.
