Brookhaven is a borough of about 8,300 residents that sits right next to Chester, with PA 352 running through its center as Edgmont Avenue. If water is spreading through a Brookhaven house, condo or apartment right now, call the Chester dispatch line. It is open 24 hours a day, and the crew works out of the same city as your neighbors on the other side of the line.

Most of Brookhaven was built after World War II. Incorporated in 1945 with 672 residents and about 150 homes, the borough grew to more than 3,600 homes, apartments and condominiums. That age profile shapes the water problems below.

Postwar homes and the water problems that come with age

Brookhaven grew fast. The population went from about 1,000 in 1950 to thousands by 1960, as stock farms that had been consolidated under one owner were sold to developers. Many houses on those streets are now six or seven decades old. Original water heaters have long been replaced, but supply lines behind walls, washer hookups and bathroom drains may not have been.

Age matters because slow failures are common in older plumbing. A pinhole in a line behind a wall can run for weeks before a stain appears. The burst pipe and wall and drywall pages explain how those leaks are found and dried.

When water is spreading in Brookhaven

Condos and apartments add a second layer. A leak upstairs reaches the unit below through the floor structure, so one failed fixture can damage several homes. If you live in a multi-unit building, tell the property manager at once and photograph the damage before anything is cleaned.

Ceiling stains in the unit below an apartment are the classic sign. The ceiling water damage repair page covers what to do about a sagging or dripping ceiling. If the building is a business, see commercial water damage restoration.

Creeks, mills and what they mean for your address

Chester Creek and Ridley Creek powered the mills that gave this area its early industry, and the creeks still shape the land. Whether your property sits in a mapped flood zone is something to check, not assume. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center lets you search by address, and your insurer can tell you whether flood damage is covered, which standard homeowners policies often exclude.

If water comes from outside, the flood damage cleanup page explains how contaminated water is handled differently from a plumbing leak.

Around Brookhaven: Cambridge Shopping Center, Scott Park and the schools

Brookhaven has a mix of homes and small businesses. The Cambridge Shopping Center opened in 1972 and is anchored by a large hardware retailer, which gives you an idea of the commercial buildings nearby, many with flat roofs and shared walls. A roof drain failure over a storefront needs fast action, since water moves across ceiling tiles quickly.

Penn-Delco School District serves the borough, and Coebourn Elementary sits in town. When storms hit, school and municipal buildings need the same drying care as homes. Ask the property owner or district facilities staff to call dispatch for any water event in a larger building.

Questions from Brookhaven homeowners

Do you cover every ZIP code in Brookhaven?

Brookhaven itself is ZIP code 19015. Service also reaches the neighboring communities of Media, in 19063, 19065 and 19091, Marcus Hook in 19061 and Garnet Valley in 19060, from the PA 352 corridor out to the borough edges.

Is emergency water removal available in Brookhaven?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day and same-day response is offered when a crew is available. Tell the dispatcher your address, the source of the water and whether it is near electrical equipment.

Who should I call if my apartment ceiling is dripping from upstairs?

Call your property manager first and then dispatch. Photograph the ceiling and the source, and do not stand under a bulge. A sagging ceiling can fall without warning.

Do postwar houses in Brookhaven need special care after water damage?

Older houses can have plaster walls, older insulation and original flooring, which dry differently from new construction. A moisture meter reading before any wall is closed is the safest approach.
