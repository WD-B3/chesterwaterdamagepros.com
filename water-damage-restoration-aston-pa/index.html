Along PA 452 and Pennell Road

PA 452 follows Pennell Road north and south through the township and is its only numbered highway. Most homes are on the quieter roads that branch off it, and the township maintains about 54 miles of its own streets. Aston grew fastest in the 1940s through the 1960s, so many houses are mid-century construction.

After the water is out in a mid-century Aston house

Once standing water is removed, the work shifts to drying and deciding what stays. Mid-century houses often have drywall or plaster over block basement walls, hardwood or vinyl over plywood subfloors and wood trim fixed with nails. Each of those dries at a different pace, so readings are taken on every material instead of one spot.

  • Block walls hold water and release it slowly, which is why basement dehumidification can run for days.
  • Plywood subfloor under vinyl or tile can trap moisture, and a meter reading tells you whether to open it.
  • Wood trim and baseboards are often removed and reset so air can reach the wall base.
  • Insulation in a wet cavity is usually replaced, not dried.

Ask for the readings and the equipment log, and keep them for your insurer. The structural drying page explains what a good log looks like, and EPA guidance is to dry water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth.
