What the first 24 to 72 hours look like

The early hours decide how much of a house can be saved. The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas and items within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, and professional drying is planned around that window.

WindowWhat mattersWhat you can do
First hoursStop the source and remove standing water.Close the valve, photograph the damage, call dispatch.
Day oneStart air movement and dehumidification, remove soaked pad and contents.Lift belongings off wet floors and keep people away from electrical hazards.
Day twoOpen wet cavities and confirm with a meter that materials are drying.Call your insurer and keep every receipt.
Day three onMonitor readings daily until materials reach the dry standard.Ask for readings and an equipment log.

Drying ends when readings reach target, not when surfaces feel dry. The [[structural-drying-dehumidification|structural drying]] page explains the equipment and the logs.

What affects the cost of water damage restoration

We do not post prices because no honest number exists until someone sees the water. These are the factors that move a job up or down.

FactorWhy it changes the job
Water typeClean, gray and contaminated water need different protection and disposal.

Questions from Chester homeowners

What should I do first when water gets into my home?

Stop the source if it is safe to do so, keep clear of electrical equipment and call the dispatch line. Photograph the damage before cleanup, because insurers ask for it. Then lift belongings off wet floors and wait for instructions from the dispatcher.

Is the dispatch line really open at night and on weekends?

Yes. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available, and the dispatcher will tell you plainly what to expect for your situation.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer before you decide, and keep photos and receipts.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

A real price depends on how much water there is, what it touched and how long it sat. Dispatch can explain the process and the factors, and scope and price are explained before work starts.

How can I tell whether a company is doing the job properly?

Ask for moisture readings, photos and an equipment log. Ask whether the crew works to ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage and S520 for mold. A company that dries without measuring is guessing.

Which towns do you serve?

Chester and nearby Delaware County towns, including Brookhaven, Wallingford, Swarthmore, Ridley Park and Aston. The service area page lists the ZIP codes for each.
