Call (856) 702-0892 for water damage in Chester, PA and nearby towns. The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day, including weekends and holidays. There is no web form, because a phone call gets a plan started sooner than an email does.

What to tell the dispatcher

  1. Where the water is coming from and whether it is stopped.
  2. How deep it is and which rooms it has reached.
  3. Whether it is near outlets, a panel or appliances.
  4. How long it has been there.
  5. Whether you have photographed the damage and contacted your insurer.

Having these answers ready speeds the call. If you do not know something, say so. The dispatcher can talk you through checking.

Safety comes first

If there is sparking, buzzing, a smell of gas or water near a panel, leave the area and call 911 or the utility before calling us. If sewage is involved, keep people and pets away from the water.

Do not wait for a callback to protect your belongings. Lift items off wet floors and photograph the damage while you wait.

Before you call

Your insurance policy number and the name of your insurer are useful to have nearby, though not required. Check the services page if you want to understand what kind of loss you have.
