How the services fit together

Almost every job moves through the same stages: stop the source, remove standing water, dry the structure and rebuild what could not be saved. The [[water-extraction|water extraction]] and [[structural-drying-dehumidification|structural drying]] pages describe the shared core. The cause-specific pages explain what is different about each source.

Pipes and plumbing

Burst Pipe Water Damage

Shut-off steps, extraction and drying after a supply line splits.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage

Thawing risks, split pipes and the water that follows a freeze.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup

Tank failures, slow leaks at the base and wet utility rooms.

Slab Leak Water Damage

Hidden leaks under floors and what they do to flooring and walls.

Appliances and HVAC

Appliance Leak Water Damage

Washers, dishwashers and refrigerator lines that leak into floors.

AC Leak Water Damage

Clogged drain lines and overflow pans that wet ceilings and closets.

Kitchen Water Damage

Cabinets, flooring and walls soaked by sink, dishwasher or line failures.

Weather and building envelope

Ceiling Water Damage Repair

Stains, sagging drywall and wet insulation below a leak.

Storm Water Damage

Wind-driven rain, roof openings and wet interiors after storms.

Flood Damage Cleanup

Contaminated flood water, removal of porous materials and drying.

Basement Water Damage

Standing water, wet block walls and damp finished spaces below grade.

Crawl Space Water Damage

Wet insulation, standing water and moisture under the house.

Water removal and drying

Water Extraction

Pumps and extractors that remove standing water fast.

Structural Drying

Air movers, dehumidifiers and daily moisture readings.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Contaminated water from drains and toilets, handled with protection.

Floors and walls

Hardwood Floor Water Damage

Cupping, crowning and deciding what can be dried in place.

Carpet Water Damage

Extraction, pad removal and drying of wet carpet.

Wall and Drywall Water Damage

Wet cavities, drywall removal and drying before rebuild.

Commercial

Commercial Water Damage

Shops, offices and rental properties with water losses.

Mold, secondary to water damage

Mold Remediation

Moisture control, containment and removal where growth has started.
