Privacy Policy

What we receive when you call, email or visit this website.

☎ (856) 702-0892

This policy explains what information Chester Water Damage Pros receives and how it is used. It applies to this website and to calls to our dispatch line. Last updated 2026-11-09.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we receive your phone number and the details you share, such as your address and a description of the water damage.

We use this information to understand your situation, arrange a response and improve how the line is run.

Email

If you email contact@chesterwaterdamagepros.com, we receive your email address and your message. We use it to reply.

This website

The site has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows maps from Google Maps, so your browser may contact those services when a page loads. Their own policies apply to that traffic.

Sharing with responders

To arrange a response, we pass the details of your request to the restoration professionals who may respond to you. We ask them to use it only to help with your request.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by emailing us. You can tell the dispatcher at the start of a call if you have a question about recording.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@chesterwaterdamagepros.com or raised by phone at (856) 702-0892.
