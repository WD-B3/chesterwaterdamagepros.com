Downtown Chester is a mixed-use area, and the city calls it Overtown. The Vision 2035 comprehensive plan says Overtown is Chester's central business district and that the Chester Transportation Center is a regional asset there.

A water loss downtown can affect a ground-floor shop, an office above it and an apartment on the top floor at once. If water is spreading through a downtown building now, call the dispatch line and tell the dispatcher which floors are wet.

A mixed-use district

The plan describes work to fill Overtown's vacant storefronts and to improve traffic and parking, which tells us the district has both active and empty commercial space. A directory of local housing styles lists row homes as the most common home style in Downtown Chester.

We have not found a count of apartments or residents, so we make no claim about how many people live here. The practical point is that many downtown buildings combine several uses under one roof.

When one leak reaches several floors

In a multi-story building, water moves down through floor structure, pipe chases and wall cavities. A failed line on the third floor can wet a ceiling on the second and a shop below that.

  • Shut off water to the unit and, if you manage the building, to the riser.
  • Warn occupants of the floors below before they find the damage themselves.
  • Photograph each affected unit separately, with dates.
  • Keep tenants, owners and managers in writing about who reported what.

Commercial losses raise questions about lease terms and business interruption. The commercial water damage restoration page covers them in more detail.

Vacant and underused space

A vacant storefront can hold a slow leak for weeks because nobody is there to see it. A roof drain, a failed supply line or a broken window can let water in unnoticed.

If you own or manage a vacant downtown space, a regular walk-through with a flashlight at ceiling and wall corners is cheap protection. Check basements and mechanical rooms, which are the places water collects first.

The first hour in a downtown building

  • Stop the sourceClose the nearest valve. In a shared building, locate the riser shutoff before you need it.
  • Mind the powerDo not stand in water to reach a panel. Call the utility for sparking or buzzing.
  • Protect recordsLift paper files, inventory and electronics above the waterline.
  • DocumentTake photos and video of each floor before anything is moved.

If the water came from a drain or sewer line, treat it as contaminated and keep people out until it has been assessed.

Drying a multi-story building

Drying a downtown building means more than running fans. Crews map moisture in each room, place air movers and dehumidifiers floor by floor and log readings daily, because one dry floor can sit above one that is still wet.

The structural drying page explains the equipment, and the water extraction page covers pumping. Ask for a written scope that lists each unit separately.

Insurance and responsibility in shared buildings

Who pays for what depends on your policy and your lease. A building owner's policy, a tenant's policy and a business policy may each respond to part of a loss. Read yours or ask your insurer.

The Pennsylvania Insurance Department advises taking pictures or video before cleanup and finding out the total repair cost before accepting a settlement.

Downtown Chester: creek, levee and flood questions

Chester Creek meets the Delaware River inside the city, and the comprehensive plan notes that Deshong and Eyre Parks are bisected by the creek and calls for the Army Corps of Engineers to evaluate the Chester Creek levee. The plan does not say which downtown properties, if any, are at risk.

Downtown flood-zone status is not something we have confirmed. Search the building's address on FEMA's map, and ask the insurer whether flood damage falls under the policy.

Questions from Downtown Chester owners and tenants

Do you handle storefronts and offices as well as homes?

Yes. Call and describe the building, the floors affected and the source. The dispatcher explains the first steps, and scope and price are explained before work starts.

What is Overtown?

It is the city's name for Chester's central business district, according to the Vision 2035 comprehensive plan. We use Downtown Chester and Overtown as landmarks, not as official boundaries.

A leak upstairs wet my shop. Who do I call first?

Stop the source if you can, then call dispatch and your insurer. Tell the building owner or manager in writing and photograph your own damage.

Can water damage in a vacant unit be urgent?

Yes. Water keeps moving through a building whether or not someone is there, so call as soon as you find it.
