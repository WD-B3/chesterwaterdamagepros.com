Sun Village is the name used for the Morton Avenue part of Chester. The city's Vision 2035 comprehensive plan says the Morton Avenue Corridor extends between Ridley Creek and the Delaware River, and that it takes in residential neighborhoods with a mix of row houses and twin houses in varying condition.

If water is spreading through a Sun Village house right now, call the dispatch line. The dispatcher asks where the water began, how deep it is and whether it is near electrical equipment, then explains what to do while a crew is arranged.

Where this page applies

The plan says the 28-acre Sun Village Park is near the corridor, and Morton Avenue carries U.S. Route 13 through this part of the city. Those two landmarks are the clearest way to place the area. We have not found official block boundaries for Sun Village, so we do not draw any.

Some directories use the name Sun Hill for streets in the same part of Chester. We treat that as a separate label and have not combined the two, since we could not confirm how residents use either name.

Rows and twins in varying condition

The plan's wording, row houses and twin houses in varying condition, is a general description. It does not say which houses have basements, what pipes they contain or how often water enters them. We do not guess at those details.

What can be said safely is how shared-wall houses behave when water gets in. A row house shares walls with both neighbors, so a leak near a party wall can reach a home you do not own.

  • Report the loss to your insurer and tell the adjacent households.
  • Check the shared wall at the base, where water settles first.
  • Look at the ceiling below any upstairs bathroom or kitchen.
  • Ask for readings on both sides of a party wall before it is closed.

Roof and window leaks after heavy rain

Rain finds the weak point in a roof, a flashing detail or a window frame. In a row or twin house, a roof leak often shows first as a stain on an upstairs ceiling, then spreads along framing toward a shared wall.

Put a bucket under active drips, move belongings and photograph the stain. Do not stand under a sagging ceiling. The ceiling water damage repair page explains what happens when drywall holds water.

If water comes in from outside

Chester sits low beside the Delaware River, and this corridor lies between the river and Ridley Creek. That is geography, not a record of flooding. Whether any part of Sun Village sits in a mapped flood zone is unknown to us, so check the exact address on FEMA's flood map.

Floodwater and storm runoff can carry soil and bacteria, and it can hide electrical hazards. Keep out of it, keep children and pets away, and read the flood damage cleanup page before deciding what to do.

First steps and safety

  • Water supplyClose the fixture valve or the main if you can reach it without standing in water.
  • ElectricityLeave breakers alone if the floor is wet. Call 911 or the utility for sparking or buzzing.
  • ContentsLift small items onto dry surfaces. Do not strain to move heavy furniture.
  • RecordsTake photos and video before cleanup, and keep receipts for emergency purchases.

Household fans and ordinary vacuums are not enough for sewage or contaminated water. Those jobs need protective equipment and proper disposal.

Extraction, drying and what you should receive

After the free water is pumped or extracted, moisture is still inside drywall, plaster, subfloor and framing. Air movers and dehumidifiers remove it over several days while a crew logs readings. See water extraction for the first stage.

Ask for a written scope that lists rooms, equipment and readings. Sudden and accidental damage is often covered by homeowners insurance, and flooding often needs separate coverage, so check your policy and ask your insurer.

Sun Village, Chester: what we verified about drainage

The plan names the Stormwater Authority of Chester and treats combined sewer overflows as a citywide issue. It does not report backups at any specific address, and neither do we.

If sewage or drain water comes up inside your home, stay out of it, tell the dispatcher and report the problem to your sewer utility. Ask the City of Chester Licenses and Inspections office what permits apply before major rebuild work.

Questions from Sun Village homeowners

What streets count as Sun Village?

We use the Morton Avenue Corridor and Sun Village Park as landmarks. The city's plan describes the corridor as running between Ridley Creek and the Delaware River, and we have not found official block boundaries.

Is Sun Village a flood zone?

Unknown. Flood zones are mapped by address, not by neighborhood name, so check yours on FEMA's online map.

Is a row house leak my neighbor's problem or mine?

Water follows the building, not the property line. Report your own damage, tell the neighbor and let each household's insurer decide coverage. Do not enter a neighbor's home without permission.

Can you work on a house in varying condition?

Call and describe what you see. The dispatcher explains the first steps, and scope and price are explained before work starts.
