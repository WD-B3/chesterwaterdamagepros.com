Emergency Slab Leak Water Damage in Chester, PA

If you can hear water running when everything is off, a hidden leak may be under the floor.

☎ (856) 702-0892

A slab leak is a leak in a water line that runs under or through a concrete slab. It is hidden by design, which is why people notice it by its symptoms: a hissing sound, a warm spot on the floor, a high water bill or damp carpet with no obvious source. Slab leak water damage cleanup in Chester, PA starts after the line is located and stopped.

This page covers the water damage side of a slab leak. A leak detection plumber finds and repairs the line. If water is visibly rising or spreading on a floor, treat it as an emergency, and our 24/7 dispatch line is open.

Signs of a slab leak

People rarely search for the trade name. They search for what they see. Typical signs include a warm patch on the floor, the sound of running water when every fixture is off, a water meter that keeps moving, damp carpet or baseboards, a musty smell, and a bill that jumps with no change in use.

To check the meter, shut off every fixture and look at the leak indicator on the meter. If it moves, water is going somewhere. Then close the main valve. If the meter stops, the leak is on your side of the line.

Once a plumber has fixed the line, drying of flooring and walls follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard for professional water damage restoration.

Does my house have a slab?

Not every house does. Slab-on-grade foundations put plumbing lines inside or beneath concrete. Homes with full basements and crawl spaces usually run lines overhead or through those spaces where they are easier to see. Additions, garages and some commercial buildings are more likely to sit on slabs.

Check your property record or ask a plumber before assuming a slab leak. If your home has a basement, the better pages to read are burst pipe water damage and basement water damage.

What to do when you suspect a slab leak

  • Shut the mainThis stops the damage while you arrange a leak detection plumber.
  • Move belongingsLift furniture off wet or warm flooring.
  • Check the water heaterA warm floor spot near the heater is sometimes a hot water line leak.
  • PhotographPictures of damp flooring, the meter and any stains help with an insurance claim.

Cleanup and drying after the line is fixed

Once the plumber has repaired the line, the crew checks what the water reached. Carpet and pad usually come up. Wood flooring may cup and buckle. Drywall at the base of walls takes in water by wicking, so baseboards are removed and the lowest drywall is checked with a meter.

Concrete itself holds water and releases it slowly, which is why humidity needs controlling with LGR dehumidifiers. See structural drying for the process, hardwood floor and carpet for finish repair.

Reading the meter and the bill

A water bill that climbs with no change in habits is a clue, though bills are a slow signal. The meter is faster. Turn off every fixture, appliance and ice maker, then look at the leak indicator on the meter. A moving dial or flashing indicator means water is going somewhere. If you are not sure where your meter is, check your bill for your water utility and ask them.

A leak detection plumber uses acoustic listening equipment, pressure testing and sometimes thermal imaging to locate the line before any concrete is cut. The repair may be a spot repair under the slab, a reroute through the walls or attic, or a replacement of the line. That is a plumbing decision, and the plumber can explain the options.

Tell your insurer about the leak early. Pennsylvania Insurance Department guidance is to contact your insurer promptly and to take photos or video before cleanup. Ask whether the policy covers the cost of reaching the pipe as well as the water damage that followed.

Cost factors and insurance

  • Run time. Hidden leaks often run for weeks.
  • Flooring. Large carpeted or wood areas drive cost.
  • Contents. Furniture on wet flooring can be affected.
  • Plumbing access. Breaking the slab is a plumber and flooring cost, separate from water damage work.

Whether hidden leaks are covered depends on the policy. Sudden damage may be, and ongoing leakage often is not. Check your policy or ask your insurer. Call (856) 702-0892 to talk it through.

Common questions

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Chester?

Yes. If water is spreading on a floor, call. Dispatch answers 24 hours a day and same-day response is offered when a crew is available.

Do you find slab leaks?

A leak detection plumber finds and repairs the line. We take over for water removal, drying and restoration.

How do I tell a slab leak from a surface leak?

A hissing sound, a warm floor spot or a moving meter with all fixtures off points below the surface.

Will water under a slab cause mold?

It can if materials stay wet. Drying and moisture checks reduce that risk.

Can the slab be dried?

Concrete dries slowly. Dehumidifiers and air movers speed it, and moisture readings confirm progress.
