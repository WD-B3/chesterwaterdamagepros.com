Kitchen Water Damage in Chester, PA

Cabinets trap water against subfloor and walls. Find out what is wet behind the toe kick.

☎ (856) 702-0892

The kitchen concentrates water sources: a sink supply and drain, a dishwasher, a refrigerator line and often a floor of tile or laminate over a wood subfloor. A slow drip under the sink can wet the cabinet floor, swell particleboard and spread into the subfloor before anyone sees it.

Kitchen water damage restoration in Chester, PA covers the cabinet and sink area, water in cabinetry, and the finishes around them. Appliance failures have their own page, and this page focuses on cabinets and the sink zone.

Signs of water under a sink or in cabinets

Common signs are a swollen cabinet floor, peeling laminate, a musty smell, a soft spot in the floor near the sink, and dark stains on the underside of the countertop. Drips from a supply line connector, a loose trap or a cracked drain are the usual causes.

Particleboard cabinets swell when wet and rarely return to shape. Solid wood and plywood cabinets handle moisture better, but finishes still fail. Test by pressing the cabinet floor with your thumb. A soft spot means water has been there a while.

Drying under cabinets and behind toe kicks follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the published standard for water damage restoration, and the crew should log readings for each material.

What to do now

  • Close the angle stopsShutoff valves under the sink stop the supply.
  • Empty the cabinetTake out cleaning products and move them to dry shelves.
  • PhotographShow the cabinet floor, the pipes and the toe kick.
  • Do not run the dishwasherA leaking drain line would flood the cabinet again.

How the crew dries a kitchen

The crew extracts water, then checks the subfloor, the base of walls and the backs of cabinets with a moisture meter. Toe kicks are removed so air can reach the space beneath cabinets. Small holes may be drilled in cabinet toe kicks to dry the cavity without full demolition, and air movers and dehumidifiers run for several days.

If the cabinet cannot be saved, it is removed and replaced. The process mirrors the approach on the structural drying page. Dishwasher and refrigerator failures are on the appliance leak water damage page.

Floors under kitchen cabinets and in the room

Water that gets under a kitchen floor affects tile grout, laminate seams and hardwood. Wood flooring can cup and the hardwood floor water damage repair page covers that. A leak that started inside a wall behind the sink is covered under wall and drywall water damage.

Water also travels to the room below. If the kitchen sits over a basement, check the ceiling underneath for stains.

Small habits that catch kitchen leaks early

Open the sink cabinet once a month and look at the supply connections, the trap and the cabinet floor. A dry paper towel held against each connection shows a drip that your eye might miss. Run the disposal and dishwasher while watching the drain connections.

Look at the places water sneaks in from above. A failing caulk line along a backsplash, a loose faucet base or a poorly sealed window sill can drip into the cabinet below without any plumbing failure at all.

If your kitchen sits over a basement, check the ceiling beneath the sink and dishwasher. A stain there often means the leak has already gone through the floor.

Cost factors and insurance

  • Cabinet material. Particleboard rarely survives. Plywood and solid wood sometimes do.
  • Countertops. Stone or laminate countertops may need removal to replace cabinets.
  • Flooring. Continuous flooring can mean larger repairs.
  • Duration. A slow leak that ran for months is bigger than a single overflow.

Sudden leaks are often covered by homeowners insurance, and long-term seepage often is not. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and keep receipts. Call (856) 702-0892 to discuss it. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

Common questions

Can swollen cabinets be saved?

Sometimes, if the water was clean and found fast. Particleboard rarely recovers once swollen.

Do I have to remove the countertop?

Not always. Crews often work from the toe kick and access holes. Replacing cabinets under a stone countertop does require lifting it.

Do you handle kitchen water emergencies at night or on weekends in Chester?

Dispatch is open 24 hours a day. If a supply line is spraying, close the valve and call.

What if the leak is behind the dishwasher?

Pull the unit forward if it is safe and check the floor. The appliance leak page explains that case.

How do I know the cabinet is dry?

A moisture meter reading compared with a dry cabinet in the same kitchen.
